# Demonstrating AgroShift

1. Open the home screen and choose **Try an example farm**. Review the three visible crop cards. The example label identifies fictional field inputs.
2. Open a crop's **View details**. Expand **Evidence and sources**. For groundnut or maize, compare an explicitly selected FAO land-map water scenario.
3. Compare two or three crops and save the farmer report.
4. Choose **My Farm → Add a farm**. Choose Kenya and the saved Nairobi location. Leave measurements blank, select March through July, then find crops. Review the general references for this location.
5. Open **Explore → Rotation comparison** with the Sri Lankan example. Open **What-if scenarios**, select persistent waterlogging and inspect which rotations are excluded.
6. In **Explore → Data sources**, inspect soil estimates, crop calendars or national statistics. Their source, spatial scope and retrieval details remain visible.

7. Open **My Farm**, name the field and choose **Save farm**. Export the profile, inspect **Assessment history**, then add another field. Anonymous saves last for this session.

The portable demo uses 34 archived NASA histories and the database release. Live place search and extra provider requests need internet access. An outage retains matching archived records when available and otherwise shows an error.

Public deployment and competition submission have separate requirements. The code has been developed before any official eligibility determination. Recheck current NASA Space Apps rules for pre-event development, reuse, AI disclosure and the intended event before submitting.
