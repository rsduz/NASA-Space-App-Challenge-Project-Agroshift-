* Farmer preferences.

## Step 3 — Compatibility Analysis

* Check temperature constraints.
* Evaluate soil compatibility where information exists.
* Consider the potential benefits and limitations of legume rotations.

## Step 4 — Multi-Criteria Assessment

Research and implement a transparent multi-criteria decision method.

Consider:

* Climate compatibility.
* Water compatibility.
* Soil compatibility.
* Rotation characteristics.
* Farmer priorities.

Use documented assumptions and sensible weighting methods.

## Step 5 — Explainable Ranking

For each candidate rotation:

* Display its relative ranking.
* Show component-level assessments.
* Explain the main advantages.
* Show important limitations.
* Identify missing inputs.
* Provide relevant scientific references.

## Step 6 — Sensitivity Analysis

Where feasible, demonstrate how rankings change when:

* Water availability changes.
* Farmer priorities change.
* Soil information changes.
* Climate assumptions change.
