## How the sources enter the application

**GAEZ.** The official [download notebook](https://github.com/un-fao/gaezv5/tree/main/GAEZ-v5-Platform/Data-Access-Notebooks) identifies files using the RES05 workbook. The refresh script checks the listing, fetches each selected file and validates its geometry and values against the [FAO class legend](https://data.apps.fao.org/gismgr/api/v2/catalog/workspaces/GAEZ-V5/styles/SIX/sld). The dataset covers all land in the cell, averaging historical 2001–2020 AgERA5 conditions at 5 arc minutes (about 10 km at the equator). Six crops have rainfed and irrigated low-input layers. Codes 1–8 are ordered model classes. Codes 0 and 9 withhold the result. They represent missing coverage and water. The reader uses the native WGS84 grid and the cell containing the query. No potential yield enters the calculation. Finer 30 arc-second layers, other management levels and modelled calendars exist in the larger catalogue. [Dataset and licence](https://data.apps.fao.org/catalog/dataset/514c92d5-9e01-4c70-814a-80ea1ca9fe6a) · [Model guide](https://github.com/un-fao/gaezv5/wiki/21.-GAEZ-v5-Platform-User-Guide)

**Country context.** The separate [GAEZ crop summary](https://data.apps.fao.org/catalog/dataset/crop-summary-gaez) provides a 178-row wheat sample (177 distinct ISO3 codes) for one historical management scenario. The included function retains its land cover, exclusion, period, water regime, zone and climate source. It reads only the area in the “Suitable” class in square kilometres. FAOSTAT uses [the published bulk catalogue](https://bulks-faostat.fao.org/production/datasets_E.json). A command-line refresh streams the QCL table once and writes a small country extract. Kenya's 2023 maize production is recorded as **4,285,206 tonnes**, FAOSTAT flag A (official figure). This is a national total. Source attribution: FAO. 2025. FAOSTAT, Crops and livestock products. Accessed 8 November 2026. [QCL](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/QCL) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**Soil.** The service samples a 5 × 5 WGS84 output window, checks its CRS and affine origin and reads the requested pixel. For Anuradhapura at 8.3114, 80.4037, the archived pH estimate was 5.8. The pixel represents a regional model with spatial uncertainty. Estimates, including unknown values, are returned separately from measured field pH. The Research view can display a result without changing the farm. WebDAV and global rasters are alternatives for offline GIS processing. [Measurement units](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/SoilGrids_faqs_01.html) · [WCS usage](https://docs.isric.org/globaldata/soilgrids/WCS_from_Python.html) · [Source licence](https://isric.org/explore/soilgrids)

**ECOCROP.** FAO retired the original system around 2015 and now makes the database discoverable through GAEZ. Species-wide annual precipitation bounds require annual measurements. Farm-season rainfall, geographic occurrence and cultivar-specific performance describe different conditions. The [HTML search](https://ecocrop.apps.fao.org/ecocrop/srv/en/cropSearchForm) supports optimal/absolute filters. No automated HTML extraction is used. For a legitimately obtained, attributable release, combine reviewed records with the retained records in `data/global_catalogue.json` format, preserve units, applicability, version and retrieval date, and run `python -m scripts.knowledge_db import-global --file new_release.json --sqlite .cache/trial.sqlite3`. Mark unchecked records `pending` until a reviewer can verify the source wording. There is no preloaded extract of all species. [FAO documentation](https://www.fao.org/geospatial/data-and-tools/data-portals/ecocrop/) · [Database workflow](KNOWLEDGE_DATABASE.md)

**Reliability and offline use.** Provider calls are made on demand. Exact query keys, bounded in-memory caches, per-key locks, a one-day cache lifetime and rate-limit cooldowns reduce repeated requests. New public reference calls use at most two attempts with connect/read timeouts and a 1 MB application response cap. A failure can use only a previously parsed response or a matching dated archive. A SHA-256 digest accompanies each physical snapshot. Geocoding keeps up to 128 small exact queries in memory. Its result limit is ten places. Map estimates and national statistics never enter a private farm profile as measurements. Calendar results may contain multiple zones and should be read alongside their update date.

**Refresh.** Use `python scripts/refresh_provider_data.py gaez`, `faostat`, or `samples` from the repository root in the project environment. `all` runs all three. Use a development checkout with free disk space for the FAOSTAT ZIP (~34 MB in this check). Versions, request parameters, retrieval times and checksums are saved in `data/gaez/manifest.json`, `data/faostat_qcl_sample.json`, and `data/provider_samples/`. Keep the verified artifacts with the release. Public asset rights remain with the original publishers. FAO/IIASA GAEZ v5, FAO AGROVOC official-language terms, ISRIC SoilGrids and their adaptations here carry [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
